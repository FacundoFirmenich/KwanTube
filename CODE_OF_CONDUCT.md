# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on code, methods, and evidence.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Accept correction gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disruptive trolling or bad-faith engagement.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy.
Violations may result in warning, temporary restriction, or ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers via the security/support contact defined in `SUPPORT.md`.
